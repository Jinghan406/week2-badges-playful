Emote & Chat

A farm-day interface inspired by Stardew Valley: a grass field, wooden frames, parchment notes and chunky pixels. Badges are emote bubbles, the kind that pops up over a villager's head: a bubble snaps open above the icon in four frames and keeps bobbing until you've looked. Point at the seeds and an item box follows your cursor, the way item info does in the game. Point at the watering can and it talks back in a dialogue box, typing its words out one letter at a time. Everything moves at 12 frames a second.

States

Badge, no content

Click Crops. A bubble snaps open over the turnip in four frames, overshoots a little, then bobs with a red dot inside. Click again and it pinches shut.

Badge, with content

Mail, Chest and Shop carry a count or a word inside the bubble. Same pop open, same bob, same pinch shut.

Tooltip, plain

Point at Seeds. An item box appears right under the cursor and follows it: name, category, one line of info. Move off the packet and it is gone. No action inside.

Tooltip, rich

Point at the watering can. A dialogue box opens in four steps, types its message out letter by letter (click to skip), then offers two answers. Pick one to act. It stays open while the pointer is inside it.
